## 2026-11-06

**🎉 After months of hard work, today we deliver: MianStack is officially live, free and open source!**

After months away, we're finally bringing you this carefully polished "interview gift pack" — [MianStack](https://mianstack.com/)! **Over 8,300 interview questions, 600+ reference answers, and breakdowns of 20 open-source projects**, covering Java, Python, databases, algorithms, as well as large language models, RAG, and Agent — all in one place for both technical fundamentals and AI applications.

You've saved a pile of materials, but before an interview you don't know where to start; you've followed along with projects, but when asked about technology choices and implementation details, you get stuck. Over the past few months, I've repeatedly organized real interview experiences, technical problem solutions, and project materials, hoping to help everyone spend less time searching for resources and gain more confidence when asked to "elaborate further."

- **🎯 If interviewers ask it repeatedly, focus on practicing it.** For the same question, you can trace back through related interview experiences to see which companies asked it and how it was originally phrased. High-frequency questions have sources, and review has focus.
- **🏢 Targeting a specific company? First understand their question style.** Find interview experiences by company, position, campus/social recruitment, and interview round, then practice the questions your target role has asked first.
- **📖 Answers are thorough, and follow-up questions are prepared for.** "Core answers" capture the key points for on-the-spot responses, while principles, code, diagrams, and common follow-up questions help you continue learning. Whether for last-minute cramming or systematic deep dives, there's always something to look at.
- **💼 For project interviews, find confidence directly in the source code.** Break down architecture, examine technology choices, read implementations, with resume examples and scenario-based answers. This tutorial's "Shopkeeper Data Q&A" and "Deep Research" are included too — after finishing the projects, practice how to explain them clearly.
- **🔓 All this solid material is free and open source.** Website materials are free to read, and questions plus all existing reference answers are open in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/didilili/mianstack); favorites and review markers require no registration and are saved in your current browser so you can come back and continue practicing.

**The tutorial teaches you principles and guides you through projects; MianStack helps you practice questions and prepare for interviews.** New interview experiences, new problem solutions, and new projects will continue to be added, with the goal of making this question bank more solid and more worth revisiting. If you find it useful, please give MianStack a [Star ⭐](https://github.com/didilili/mianstack), and feel free to share it with friends who are preparing for interviews!

👉 **[Go to MianStack and start practicing](https://mianstack.com/question/)** · **[See what your target company asks](https://mianstack.com/company/)** · **[Prepare for project interviews](https://mianstack.com/project/)** · **[Free and open source on GitHub](https://github.com/didilili/mianstack)**

**Tutorial and Documentation**

This update rewrites Chapter 28 and adds Chapters 29–33, using "Chinese keyword extraction" as a running case study to connect data preparation, training, evaluation, and model delivery into a complete learning path:

- [Chapter 28: "Overview and Overall Workflow of Large Language Model Fine-Tuning"](28-%E5%A4%A7%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83%E6%A6%82%E8%BF%B0%E4%B8%8E%E6%95%B4%E4%BD%93%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B.md): Starting from concrete problems, determine when fine-tuning is needed, and understand model selection, task definition, and baseline comparison.
- [Chapter 29: "Fine-Tuning Data Preparation and Conversation Templates"](29-%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83%E6%95%B0%E6%8D%AE%E5%87%86%E5%A4%87%E4%B8%8E%E5%AF%B9%E8%AF%9D%E6%A8%A1%E6%9D%BF.md): Clearly explains JSONL, Alpaca / ShareGPT, sample review, cleaning and deduplication, data splitting, and conversation templates, with a document Q&A data creation exercise.
- [Chapter 30: "Model Training Principles and Efficient Fine-Tuning"](30-%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E8%AE%AD%E7%BB%83%E5%8E%9F%E7%90%86%E4%B8%8E%E9%AB%98%E6%95%88%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83.md): Use concrete examples to understand loss, gradients, batches, learning rate, memory, and numerical precision, then learn LoRA / QLoRA, training logs, and checkpoints.
- [Chapter 31: "LLaMA-Factory Environment Setup and Fine-Tuning in Practice"](31-LLaMA-Factory%E7%8E%AF%E5%A2%83%E6%90%AD%E5%BB%BA%E4%B8%8E%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83%E5%AE%9E%E6%88%98.md): Follow the AutoDL single-GPU case to complete environment preparation, data registration, parameter configuration, training launch, log inspection, and result backup.
- [Chapter 32: "Fine-Tuning Evaluation and Model Deployment"](32-%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83%E6%95%88%E6%9E%9C%E8%AF%84%E4%BC%B0%E4%B8%8E%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E9%83%A8%E7%BD%B2.md): Compare the original and fine-tuned model responses, complete batch prediction, keyword scoring, error analysis, and regression checks, learn model merging and export, and include optional content on vLLM deployment and API calls.
- [Chapter 33: "Fine-Tuning Memory Optimization and Multi-GPU Training"](33-%E5%BE%AE%E8%B0%83%E6%98%BE%E5%AD%98%E4%BC%98%E5%8C%96%E4%B8%8E%E5%A4%9A%E5%8D%A1%E8%AE%AD%E7%BB%83.md): Learn how to troubleshoot insufficient memory, compare memory usage and time across different configurations, and understand the three parallelism methods, DeepSpeed ZeRO, and the trade-offs when trial-running large language models.
